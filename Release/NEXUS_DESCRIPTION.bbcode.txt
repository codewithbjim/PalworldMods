[center][size=6][b]Perfect Placement[/b][/size]
[i]Freeze the preview. Walk around it. Place it precisely.[/i][/center]

Freeze a building preview in place, walk around it, and make precise positional and rotational adjustments before handing control back to Palworld.

Perfect Placement is for the moment when vanilla placement is almost right—but the camera, terrain, or snap system will not let you put a piece exactly where you want it.

[line]
[size=5][b]Highlights[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Freeze the preview.[/b] Keep the selected build piece fixed in world space while your character and camera remain free.
[*][b]Precise movement.[/b] Nudge the piece forward, backward, left, right, up, or down using configurable centimeter increments.
[*][b]Controlled rotation.[/b] Rotate around the captured build-piece pivot instead of fighting the vanilla camera trace.
[*][b]Instant reset.[/b] Return the preview to the exact position and rotation it had when frozen.
[*][b]Eyedropper.[/b] Copy the build piece under the cursor into the active preview.
[*][b]Configurable controls.[/b] Configure Freeze, Copy, and all eleven adjustment actions through DarnMenu, including mouse buttons and Ctrl/Alt/Shift chords.
[*][b]Experimental gamepad controls.[/b] Freeze, copy, move, rotate, change step size, reset, and unfreeze with configurable controller chords.
[*][b]Palworld keycap UI.[/b] The on-screen guides use Palworld's stock keyboard, mouse, and controller keycaps and reflect the configured bindings.
[*][b]Scoped behavior.[/b] Perfect Placement only edits the temporary preview. Final construction remains on Palworld's normal validation and placement path.
[/list]

[line]
[size=5][b]Requirements[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Palworld on Windows
[*]A Palworld-compatible UE4SS installation
[*]DarnMenu
[/list]

[b]Perfect Placement contains both a UE4SS Lua mod and a Logic Mod .pak. Install both parts.[/b]

[line]
[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

[list=1]
[*]Install a Palworld-compatible version of UE4SS.
[*]Download Perfect Placement.
[*]Extract the archive into the Palworld installation folder containing [b]Pal[/b].
[*]Allow folders to merge.
[/list]

The installed files should end up at:

[code]Pal/Binaries/Win64/UE4SS/Mods/PerfectPlacement/
Pal/Content/Paks/LogicMods/PerfectPlacement.pak[/code]

If your UE4SS build still uses [b]mods.txt[/b], add:

[code]PerfectPlacement : 1[/code]

[line]
[size=5][b]Keyboard and mouse defaults[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Freeze / unfreeze preview:[/b] Middle mouse
[*][b]Move horizontally:[/b] Numpad 8 / 2 / 4 / 6
[*][b]Move up / down:[/b] Numpad 3 / 1
[*][b]Rotate:[/b] Numpad 7 / 9
[*][b]Decrease / increase movement step:[/b] Numpad - / +
[*][b]Reset to frozen transform:[/b] Numpad 5
[*][b]Copy targeted build piece:[/b] Shift + Middle mouse
[/list]

The key guide appears only while a live construction preview is available.
Mouse bindings are ignored unless Palworld has an active construction preview, so normal middle-mouse Pal commands remain unaffected.
Perfect Placement appears under [b]Escape > Mod Options[/b] for Freeze, Copy,
and all eleven adjustment bindings. DarnMenu renders complete chords with
Palworld's stock keyguide textures, captures mouse buttons 1-5, and provides
keycap toggles for any combination of Ctrl, Alt, and Shift.

[size=5][b]Experimental gamepad defaults[/b][/size]

[list]
[*][b]Freeze / unfreeze preview:[/b] L3
[*][b]Copy targeted build piece while unfrozen:[/b] L3 + D-pad Down
[*][b]Move left / right while frozen:[/b] D-pad Left / Right
[*][b]Move forward / back while frozen:[/b] D-pad Up / Down
[*][b]Raise / lower while frozen:[/b] LT + D-pad Up / Down
[*][b]Rotate left / right while frozen:[/b] LB / RB
[*][b]Decrease / increase movement step while frozen:[/b] LT + D-pad Left / Right
[*][b]Reset to frozen transform:[/b] R3
[/list]

Controller chords are configured under gamepad.bindings in Scripts/config.lua.
Frozen D-pad actions may use LT, RT, or both triggers. The controller guide
resolves Palworld's stock keyguide textures at runtime.

[b]Experimental:[/b] The author does not have a gamepad, so 0.2.0-beta.3 has not been
tested on physical controller hardware. Controller users are invited to test
in a disposable world and report the controller model, Steam Input status,
configured chord, reproduction steps, and relevant UE4SS log lines.

[line]
[size=5][b]Notes and limitations[/b][/size]

[list]
[*]Vertical movement is clamped from 25 cm below to 650 cm above the initially frozen position—an upward range of two standard wall levels.
[*]Install the mod on each client that wants to use the placement controls.
[*]Test new mod versions in a disposable world before using an important save.
[*]Gamepad support is experimental and may require controller-specific fixes.
[*]Bindings may become intermittent when another mod or UE4SS's built-in Keybinds mod registers the same chord. Check all UE4SS and mod keybinds, then remap one of the overlapping actions.
[*]DarnMenu supports Ctrl, Alt, and Shift modifier toggles, but primary-input capture does not include arrows, Space, Enter, Tab, Escape, Backspace, punctuation keys, or mouse-wheel scrolling.
[/list]

[line]
[size=5][b]Troubleshooting[/b][/size]

[b]Nothing happens when freezing[/b]

Confirm UE4SS loaded PerfectPlacement and that [b]PerfectPlacement.pak[/b] is in [b]Pal/Content/Paks/LogicMods[/b].

[b]The guide appears but the controls do not respond[/b]

Check the UE4SS console or log for [b][PerfectPlacement][/b] errors and look for a conflicting chord in UE4SS or another installed mod.

[b]The guide is missing or incomplete[/b]

Remove older Perfect Placement .pak files before installing the current version. Do not keep two versions under different filenames.

When reporting a problem, include your Palworld version, UE4SS version, installed mod list, reproduction steps, and the relevant UE4SS log section.
